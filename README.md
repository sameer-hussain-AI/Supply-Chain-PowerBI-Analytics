# Supply Chain Executive Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Supply Chain Analytics dashboard built
in Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, orders, quantity,
product performance, category performance, and regional performance.

The goal of the project is to transform raw supply-chain data into
actionable business insights that can support **sales performance
monitoring, profitability analysis, product assortment decisions, and
regional performance evaluation**.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions such as:

-   How much total sales and profit are being generated?
-   What is the overall profit margin?
-   How are sales and profit changing over time?
-   Which categories generate the most sales and profit?
-   Which products are the top contributors to sales and profit?
-   Which products have very low sales?
-   Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
-   Where should management focus its attention to improve business
    performance?

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Overall Performance

The business generated approximately **\$36.78M in sales** and **\$3.97M
in profit**, with an overall **10.78% profit margin**.

The combination of sales, orders, quantity, and margin provides a useful
baseline for evaluating future performance.

### Sales & Profit Trend

Sales remain at a relatively similar level across the displayed
2015--2017 periods.

The dashboard shows a substantial decline in the displayed 2018 value.
Because the dashboard's date selection may represent a partial period,
**2018 should be validated before being used for a direct full-year
comparison**.

### Category Performance

**Fishing** is the leading category in the displayed sales ranking.

Category-level analysis is important because a category can have high
sales while producing a different level of profitability.

### Product Performance

The **Field & Stream 16 Gun Safe** appears among the leading products in
both sales and profit in the displayed Top 10 rankings.

The Bottom 10 Sales analysis also identifies products contributing
relatively small amounts of revenue. These products can be investigated
further using demand, inventory, profitability, and carrying-cost
information.

### Regional Performance

**Western Europe** appears as the leading region in the displayed sales
ranking and also ranks strongly in profit.

However, regional sales alone should not be used to judge efficiency. A
future analysis should compare **regional profit margin, average order
value, order volume, and product mix**.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

1.  **Monitor sales and profit together** rather than focusing only on
    revenue growth.
2.  Track **profit margin by category, product, and region** to identify
    the strongest profitability drivers.
3.  Review the **bottom-performing products** for potential assortment
    optimization.
4.  Investigate the products and categories responsible for the largest
    share of total profit.
5.  Compare regions using **profit margin and average order value**, not
    just total sales.
6.  Validate the **2018 date coverage** before making year-over-year
    conclusions.
7.  Analyze the relationship between **discounting, sales, and profit**
    to identify margin pressure.
